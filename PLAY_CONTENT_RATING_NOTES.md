# Play Console content rating questionnaire — guidance

Based on what this app actually does (a professional field data-collection
tool with no games, social features, or media content).

For most questions in the IARC questionnaire, the honest answer for this
app is **"No"** — it contains none of:
- violence, blood, gore
- sexual content or nudity
- profanity or crude humor
- controlled substance references
- gambling
- user-generated content shared publicly between users
- the ability for one user to communicate with another
- unrestricted internet access/browsing within the app

Things worth double-checking honestly rather than defaulting to "No":

- **Location sharing**: the app does access precise device location.
  When asked whether the app shares the user's location, answer based on
  what actually happens — the app collects and uploads GPS coordinates
  as photo metadata, so this should be disclosed accurately here too
  (it should already match your Data Safety form answers).
- **Personal information collection**: similarly, be consistent with the
  Data Safety form — this app does collect personal information
  (names, phone numbers, ID numbers, ID photos, addresses).

The expected outcome for an app like this is the lowest rating tier in
each applicable region (e.g., "Everyone" in the US IARC system, "PEGI 3"
in Europe) — there's nothing in the app that should push it higher,
**unless you answer inconsistently with what the app actually collects**,
which can trigger a mismatch flag in review. Answer accurately rather
than picking whatever gives the lowest rating.
