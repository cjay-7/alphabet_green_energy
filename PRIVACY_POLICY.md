# Privacy Policy for Alphabet Green Energy

**Effective date:** 01/01/2023
**Last updated:** 21/08/2026

Alphabet Green Energy ("the App", "we", "us") is a field data-collection
tool used by trained agents to register and monitor beneficiaries of a
cookstove distribution program. This policy explains what data the App
collects, why, and how it is handled.

## Who this policy covers

Two groups of people have data handled by the App:

- **Agents** — the people who log in and use the App on their own
  device to do their job.
- **Beneficiaries** — the people agents visit as part of the program.
  Beneficiaries do not use the App themselves; their information is
  entered into the App by an agent, with the beneficiary's consent,
  as part of program enrollment and follow-up visits.

## Information we collect

### About agents (App users)

- **Account information**: full name, email address, phone number.
  Login credentials are managed securely by Firebase Authentication and
  are never stored by the App itself.
- **Device location**: the App accesses your device's precise (GPS)
  location while in use, to attach location metadata to photos you
  upload as part of a visit record.
- **Photographs you take**: the App uses your device's camera (via the
  system camera app) to capture the photos described below.

### About beneficiaries (entered by agents)

- **Personal information**: full name, phone number, address (including
  town, district, state, and postal code).
- **Government ID information**: ID type (e.g., Aadhar Card, Voter
  Card, PAN Card, Ration Card) and ID number, along with photographs of
  the front and back of the ID document.
- **Program-related photographs**: a photo of the beneficiary's stove,
  a photo taken with the beneficiary at the time of stove handover, and
  a signed consent form photo.
- **Household survey information** (where a survey is conducted):
  gender, household size, and cooking fuel type/usage.
- **Location metadata**: GPS coordinates are attached to uploaded
  photos to verify the visit took place at the beneficiary's location.

## Why we collect this information

We collect this information solely to operate the cookstove
distribution and monitoring program: verifying beneficiary identity and
eligibility, confirming stove delivery, tracking follow-up visits, and
reporting program outcomes to Gold Standard carbon registry

We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not sell any
of it.

## How information is stored and who can access it

Data collected by the App is transmitted over an encrypted connection
and stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore for structured data,
Cloud Storage for photos), hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure.
Access is restricted to authorized program staff.

The App also temporarily caches data on the agent's device (using local
app storage) when the device is offline, until it can be synced to our
servers.

## Data retention

Agent data is stored and removed as per usuage requirements, Agent data is removed if agent is no longer working with us.
Beneficiary data will be stored for at least 5 years until the project is live, post that the data will be erased

## Beneficiary consent

Agents obtain the beneficiary's consent, including a signed consent
form, before collecting their information. 

## Your rights

Agents may contact us at 0phlbtgreens@gmail.com to review, correct, or
request deletion of their account information. Beneficiaries, or an
agent on their behalf, may contact the same address to request review,
correction, or deletion of their program record, subject to the
program's record-keeping requirements.

## Children's privacy

The App is intended for use by adult agents and adult beneficiaries. We
do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be
reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
